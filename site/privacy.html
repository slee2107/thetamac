Privacy Policy

Thetamac. Last updated October 2026.

The short version

Thetamac does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved presets, game results and best runs are stored locally on your iPhone so the app can remember them. This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

Thetamac does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. Spoken questions are played from audio files included in the app, or by your iPhone's built-in speech if you choose the Device voice.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not connect to any server.

Apple

If you choose to share iPhone analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports. These do not identify you, and we do not collect them ourselves.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
